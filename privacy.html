Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026

Tradebook ("we", "us", "our") provides a trading journal application for logging and reviewing trades. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and how it's handled.

Information we collect

  • Account information: your email address and authentication details, collected when you sign up or sign in (including via Google Sign-In).
  • Trade data: the trades you log, including entries, exits, position size, and any notes you add.
  • Usage data: basic technical information such as device type and general activity within the app, used to keep the service working and secure.

How we use your information

  • To provide and operate your trading journal account.
  • To authenticate you securely (including via Google OAuth) and protect your account.
  • To process payments for paid plans, where applicable.
  • To communicate with you about your account or service updates.

How we store and protect your data

Your data is stored using Supabase, with row-level security so your trade data is only accessible to your account. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

Google Sign-In

If you sign in with Google, we receive basic profile information (your name and email address) as permitted by the scopes you approve during sign-in. We use this only to create and authenticate your Tradebook account.

Third-party services

We use trusted third-party providers to operate Tradebook, including Supabase (database and authentication), Brevo (transactional email), and Paystack (payment processing, where applicable). Each provider processes only the data necessary to perform its function.

Your rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us using the details below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Tradebook evolves. Material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
